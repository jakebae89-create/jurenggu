## Description
```
Play a game from a thousand years ago — right in your hand.

The Juryeonggu (酒令具) is a 14-sided die from the Silla Kingdom, unearthed at Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (Anapji) in Gyeongju, Korea. Each of its 6 square and 8 hexagonal faces is carved with a playful forfeit, and players took turns rolling it and acting out whatever came up. This app recreates it in 3D, checking the shape, face layout, and even the direction of every character against the real artifact.

■ How to play
1. Add the players (up to 14).
2. On your turn, tap "Roll".
3. When the die stops, perform the forfeit on the face that lands on top.
4. The turn passes to the next player automatically.

■ Features
· A 3D 14-sided die in the artifact's true proportions — spin it with a finger, pinch to zoom
· Haptic feedback as you throw, while it tumbles, and when it lands
· The 14 original forfeits with Chinese characters, Korean reading, and explanation
· Edit any forfeit to make your own house rules, and restore the originals anytime
· 5 themes (Classic, Hanji, Jade, Neon, Mono), light/dark mode, and brightness control
· A printable papercraft template (one A4 sheet) to build your own Juryeonggu
· Korean, English, and Japanese
· Works offline, no sign-up, everything stays on your device

■ Example forfeits
· 禁聲作儛 (Geumseong-jakmu) — Dance with no music playing.
· 空詠詩過 (Gongyeong-sigwa) — Compose a poem on the spot and recite it.
· 月鏡一曲 (Wolgyeong-ilgok) — Look at the moon and sing a song.
· 自唱怪來晚 (Jachang-goeraeman) — Sing alone, then shout at the top of your lungs.

Break the ice and liven up any gathering with friends, colleagues, or family.

※ The Juryeonggu was a drinking-game die, so some forfeits mention drinking. You can rewrite them to use soft drinks or other challenges. If you drink, please do so responsibly and only if you are of legal drinking age.
```
